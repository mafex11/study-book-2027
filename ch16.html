Part 2: After Monday to mid-November

Chapter 16 of 30

Math study plan: 14 days, then weekly until the exam

When and how long: From after Monday until the exam: 30 to 45 minutes a day, following the day-by-day plan.

Prepared Wed 21 Oct 2026. It uses only the free resources already downloaded in resources/ (see 00_syllabus_and_free_resources.md section 6) plus free online videos.

Ground rules

  1. The lab interview comes first. Until it happens, research preparation (03_Research_Plan_Defense/) has priority. Math in the lab interview is unknown (../02_Interview_Strategy/00_intel_report.md). On the day before the lab interview and on the day itself, do only 15 minutes of formula flashcards and move that day's problems to the next weekend day.
  2. Talk out loud from day one. Every problem is solved on the sketchbook, with a 5 minute timer, out loud in English. Silent practice does not train the skill they score.
  3. Keep an error log. One line per mistake (format below). Redo every logged problem 3 days later and again 2 weeks later.
  4. Time budget assumed here: about 75 minutes on weekdays and 2.5 hours on Saturday and Sunday for the first 14 days, then about 5 hours a week. This is what I can really give next to my full-time job, and I add more after I leave Layerpath. The 14 days restart on Tue 27 Oct 2026, the day after the lab interview: Day 1 = Tue 27 Oct and Day 14 = Mon 9 Nov. The content and order of the days stay the same; the restarted week dates are in the weekly tracking list at the end.

Daily session template (weekday, about 75 min)

  1. 10 min: flashcards. Cover the right side of the day's sections in 01_topic_checklist_and_key_formulas.md and say them. Add one older section.
  2. 25 min: learn. One free resource for the day's topic (listed per day).
  3. 30 min: problems. The day's problems from 02_practice_problems_with_solutions.md: 5 minute timer each, out loud, on the sketchbook. Then read the solution and the "Say it out loud" box.
  4. 10 min: camera. Record one of today's problems on your phone or laptop. Watch it back at 2x speed. Is every symbol readable? Did you go silent?

Weekend session template (about 2.5 h)

Same four parts with more problems, one full lecture video, and a 12 minute mock (one LA + one C problem picked at random, with a partner if possible).

Error log format

Date Problem What went wrong (concept / arithmetic / sign / word / froze / slow) Fix in one line Redo on
(example) 22 Oct LA2 arithmetic: mixed up the order in the cross product u × v = (u2v3 - u3v2, u3v1 - u1v3, u1v2 - u2v1) 25 Oct, 8 Nov

Part 1. The next 14 days (Wed 21 Oct to Tue 3 Nov 2026)

Goal: one full pass over all 50 problems, every [HIGH] item in the checklist said out loud at least once, and three recorded mocks. This is a first pass, not mastery.

Day 1, Wed 21 Oct: setup and baseline (about 60 min)

  • Read 03_how_to_do_oral_math.md (20 min).
  • Readability test with your camera, using any paper and the thickest pen you have (10 min).
  • Baseline, cold: LA19 and C15, 5 minutes each, recorded, before reading the solutions (15 min).
  • Skim 01_topic_checklist_and_key_formulas.md and tick the [HIGH] items you can already say (15 min).
  • Order the A4 sketchbook and thick black markers. I order them online on Tue 27 Oct 2026, so they arrive in the first week of the plan.

Day 2, Thu 22 Oct: vectors (Strang Ch. 1) and review (Lang I, II)

  • Learn: 3Blue1Brown Linear Algebra Ch. 1 "Vectors", Ch. 2 "Linear combinations, span, and basis vectors", Ch. 9 "Dot products and duality". Skim the figures of OpenStax Vol. 2, 7.5 "Conic Sections".
  • Note: Lang Ch. XV (vectors) is the same material as Strang Ch. 1. You learn it once for both books.
  • Problems: LA1, LA2, C1.

Day 3, Fri 23 Oct: elimination, LU, inverses (Strang 2.1 to 2.7) and limits

  • Learn: 18.06 Lecture 2 "Elimination with matrices" and Lecture 4 "Factorization into A = LU" (read the 18.06SC summaries, watch one recitation). Paul's Notes, Calculus I, "Computing Limits".
  • Problems: LA3, LA4, C2.

Day 4, Sat 24 Oct (2.5 h): subspaces, nullspace, rank (Strang 2.5, 3.1 to 3.3) and the derivative (Lang III, IV)

  • Learn: watch 18.06 Lecture 6 "Column space and nullspace" or Lecture 7 "Solving Ax = 0: pivot variables, special solutions" in full. OpenStax Vol. 1, 3.1 "Defining the Derivative", 2.3 "The Limit Laws" (the squeeze theorem and sin x / x), 3.5 "Derivatives of Trigonometric Functions".
  • Problems: LA5, LA6, LA7, LA8, C3, C4.
  • Mock 1 (12 min, recorded): one LA and one C problem from Days 2 to 4, picked at random.

Day 5, Sun 25 Oct (2.5 h): Ax = b, the four subspaces, orthogonality (Strang 3.4 to 4.1) and derivative rules (Lang III, IV)

  • Learn: watch 18.06 Lecture 10 "The four fundamental subspaces" in full. Read Strang's free 5th edition sample section 3.5 (= 4th edition 3.6) in resources/01_Linear_Algebra_Strang/Strang_official_site/ILA5_sample_sections/. OpenStax Vol. 1, 3.3 "Differentiation Rules", 3.6 "The Chain Rule", 3.8 "Implicit Differentiation".
  • Problems: LA9, LA10, LA11, C5, C6, C7.

Day 6, Mon 26 Oct: projections and least squares (Strang 4.2, 4.3) and related rates

  • Learn: 18.06 Lectures 15 "Projections onto subspaces" and 16 "Projection matrices and least squares" (summaries plus one recitation). OpenStax Vol. 1, 4.1 "Related Rates".
  • Problems: LA12, LA13, C8.

Day 7, Tue 27 Oct: Gram-Schmidt and orthogonal matrices (Strang 4.4) and the mean value theorem (Lang V)

  • Learn: 18.06 Lecture 17 "Orthogonal matrices and Gram-Schmidt" (summary plus recitation). OpenStax Vol. 1, 4.3 "Maxima and Minima", 4.4 "The Mean Value Theorem".
  • Problems: LA14, LA15, C9.

Day 8, Wed 28 Oct: determinants (Strang Ch. 5) and curve sketching (Lang VI §1 to §4)

  • Learn: 3Blue1Brown Linear Algebra Ch. 6 "The determinant". 18.06 Lectures 18 to 20 (summaries only). OpenStax Vol. 1, 4.5 "Derivatives and the Shape of a Graph", 4.6 "Limits at Infinity and Asymptotes".
  • Problems: LA16, LA17, LA18, C10.

Day 9, Thu 29 Oct: eigenvalues and diagonalization (Strang 6.1, 6.2) and optimization (Lang VI §5)

  • Learn: 3Blue1Brown Linear Algebra Ch. 14 "Eigenvectors and eigenvalues" (and Ch. 15, the quick trick). Strang's free 5th edition sample section 6.1. OpenStax Vol. 1, 4.7 "Applied Optimization Problems".
  • Problems: LA19, LA20, C11. Compare LA19 with your Day 1 baseline recording.

Day 10, Fri 30 Oct: Jordan form idea and symmetric matrices (Strang 6.4, 6.6) and inverse functions (Lang VII)

  • Learn: 18.06 Lecture 25 "Symmetric matrices and positive definiteness" and Lecture 28 "Similar matrices and Jordan form" (summaries). OpenStax Vol. 1, 1.4 "Inverse Functions", 3.7 "Derivatives of Inverse Functions".
  • Problems: LA21, LA22, C12.

Day 11, Sat 31 Oct (2.5 h): positive definite, SVD, linear transformations (Strang 6.5, 6.7, Ch. 7) and exp, log, the fundamental theorem, substitution (Lang VIII to XI §1)

  • Learn: 18.06 Lecture 27 "Positive definite matrices and minima" (full) and Lectures 29 "Singular value decomposition" and 30 "Linear transformations and their matrices" (summaries). 3Blue1Brown Calculus Ch. 5 "What's so special about Euler's number e?" and Ch. 8 "Integration and the fundamental theorem of calculus". OpenStax Vol. 1, 3.9, 5.3 "The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus", 5.5 "Substitution".
  • Problems: LA23, LA24, LA25, C13, C14, C15.
  • Mock 2 (12 min, recorded, with a partner if possible). Partner: a friend or colleague when one is free; otherwise Claude plays the examiner in a chat session.

Day 12, Sun 1 Nov (2.5 h): integration techniques and applications (Lang X §4, XI, XII)

  • Learn: Paul's Notes, Calculus II, "Integration by Parts", "Integrals Involving Trig Functions", "Partial Fractions", "Improper Integrals" (read the worked examples). OpenStax Vol. 2, 3.1, 3.2, 3.4, 3.7, and Vol. 1, 6.1 "Areas between Curves", 6.2 "Determining Volumes by Slicing".
  • Problems: C16, C17, C18, C19, C20.

Day 13, Mon 2 Nov: Taylor's formula and series (Lang XIII, XIV)

  • Learn: 3Blue1Brown Calculus Ch. 11 "Taylor series". OpenStax Vol. 2, 6.3 "Taylor and Maclaurin Series", and skim 5.3 to 5.6 (the convergence tests).
  • Problems: C21, C22, C23.

Day 14, Tue 3 Nov: vectors in space, differential equations, review

  • Learn: skim OpenStax Vol. 3, 2.5 "Equations of Lines and Planes in Space" and 7.1 "Second-Order Linear Equations".
  • Problems: C24, C25.
  • Mock 3 (12 min): two problems picked at random from all 50, recorded.
  • Review the error log. Choose your 10 weakest problems for next week. Fill in the self-check table at the end of 00_syllabus_and_free_resources.md.

After 14 days you should be able to: say every [HIGH] item in the checklist, solve each of the 50 problems in about 5 minutes with notes closed, and talk through a mock without long silences. If not, repeat the weak days before starting Part 2.

Part 2. Weekly plan, 4 Nov 2026 to 10 Mar 2027 (lighter, about 5 h a week)

Weekly rhythm: 4 short sessions (about 45 min: flashcards, one learning resource, 2 problems out loud) and 1 long session (about 2 h: a problem set or checkpoint exam, then a 12 minute oral mock). Every week also redo the error log problems that are due.

How to use the OCW problem sets: pick 6 to 8 problems from each set that match the sections of the week, solve them on paper (not out loud, these are for depth), then check with the solutions PDF. Then pick one of them and do it again out loud on camera.

Note: the 18.06 Spring 2010 sets and exams also contain Chapter 8 (applications) questions. Chapter 8 is outside your scope. Skip those.

If you have less time

Minimum version (about 3 hours a week): keep the 4 short sessions (flashcards + 2 problems from 02 out loud), drop the OCW problem sets, and do one checkpoint exam every two weeks instead of every week. Never drop the weekly oral mock.
