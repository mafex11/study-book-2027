How to use this book

This is your prep kit in reading order, one chapter per document. Part 1 gets you ready for Monday's lab interview. Parts 2 to 4 take you to the official exam on 10 March 2027. The Reference chapters at the end are for looking things up, not for reading cover to cover.

  • Every chapter starts with when to read it and for how long, taken from your study plan.
  • Your personal answers are written in. Read them and be ready to say them in your own words.
  • Tick Mark as done at the end of a chapter. Your progress is saved on this device only (phone and PC keep separate ticks).
  • Use Next at the bottom of each page to read straight through, or open Contents.
