How to pick the slots:

  • Offer 4 to 6 slots, on at least 3 different weekdays, starting 2 or 3 days after you send the email.
  • Stay inside their working day (roughly 9:30 to 17:30 JST [INF]). Avoid 12:00 to 13:00 JST (lunch).
  • Avoid 3 Nov 2026 and 23 Nov 2026 (Japanese national holidays) and weekends.
  • Morning JST slots are early morning in India (10:00 JST = 6:30 IST). This avoids your work hours, but test your lighting and make sure you are fully awake. Offer only times you can really keep, and block any slot inside your working day with Layerpath.
  • Avoid any day you already know you cannot do, such as family events or festivals.
  • If you sent the email from Gmail, delete the paragraph about your brother's address.
