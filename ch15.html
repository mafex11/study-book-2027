Chapter 15 of 30

IPGP: decided, apply by 13 Nov if the lab supports it

When and how long: After Monday, before 13 Nov (if you choose October 2027): get the Dean's letter and the department head's report from your college, and work out your NAIST-scale GPA. Full rules: section 5.3 of the intel report.

Verified on NAIST's official page (02_Interview_Strategy/00_intel_report.md, section 5.3):

  • Application period: 14 Oct to 13 Nov 2026. You send the documents to Prof. Iida by email (CC naist-ipgp@is.naist.jp), and he writes a Letter of Acceptance.
  • Benefits: 144,000 yen a month, no tuition and no entrance fee, and airfare both ways. 4 master's places in Information Science. "Applicants' status is essentially guaranteed when they are selected as candidates by NAIST."
  • Conditions:
    • Entry is October 2027 only.
    • GPA of 2.30 or higher on NAIST's scale (marks 80 to 100 = 3, 70 to 79 = 2, 60 to 69 = 1, weighted by credits). Calculate it from your transcript.
    • You need a recommendation letter from your Dean or Principal (addressed to NAIST's President), an evaluation report from your department head, your transcript, degree certificate, passport copy and photo.
  • Important: you cannot apply for another MEXT or JASSO programme at the same time. You emailed Nagoya about their MEXT Data Science programme on 20 Oct. Decided: IPGP, so do not submit the Nagoya MEXT application.
  • What to do: at Monday's interview, ask the lab politely whether they can support an IPGP application this year (question 10 in 02_Interview_Strategy/05_questions_to_ask_them.md). At the same time, ask your college for the two letters now, because they take time.
