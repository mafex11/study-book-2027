Part 1: Before Monday (23 to 26 Oct)

  • 1. Presentation guide and scriptFriday evening: sections 1 and 2 (15 min), the script out loud with the slides, twice (30 min), and read and be ready to say your answers to Q7 (statistics at VTU), Q13 (paying for the pilot yourself), Q16 (how you counted the BrandMemory numbers) and Q20 (your own role in building Yuki) (10 min). Saturday: section 3, say Q1 to Q8 and Q17 out loud until they feel natural (1 hour). Sunday, after the mock: Q9 to Q16, Q18, Q19.
  • 2. Full script: from hello to the last slideSunday: read it out loud three times, with the slides open. Monday 08:30: once more. About 6 minutes for the slides.
  • 3. Mock interview: questions for your friendSunday: give this page to a friend. 30 minutes: your slides, then the 20 core questions; the extra ones if there is time.
  • 4. The 15-minute game planFriday evening, about 10 minutes.
  • 5. Your proposal, explained simplyFriday evening, about 45 minutes.
  • 6. Core lab papers (GUIDE 1)Saturday: only the L1 and L2 parts (30 min). Then read the L1 (arXiv) paper in full, knowing section 5 well (45 min), and L2 (arXiv) sections 4.2, 4.3, 5.1 and 7 (1 hour).
  • 7. Lab profile and peopleSaturday, about 20 minutes: sections 2.3 (Dr. Reid) and 2.4 (Dr. Ji), including Dr. Ji's likely questions.
  • 8. Dr. Ji in depthSaturday, right after the lab profile: about 1.5 hours for this and the Dr. Reid dossier together. Read sections 1, 4 and 6 first: who he is, how his work connects to your proposal, and the questions he could ask you.
  • 9. Dr. Reid in depthSaturday, right after the Dr. Ji dossier: about 1.5 hours for both dossiers together. Read sections 1, 4 and 6 first: who she is, how her work connects to your proposal, and the questions she could ask you.
  • 10. Weak points and honest answersSunday, about 45 minutes. One honest line each for: Japanese level, the IJIRAE paper vs "no publications", "most of my software with coding agents", using AI on the proposal, and statistics (#28).
  • 11. Question bank with model answersSunday, about 1 hour: only A1, A5, A7 and A10 to A13. Your decisions are written in: October 2027 (April if the lab prefers), IPGP and not Nagoya MEXT, leave Layerpath before you enrol, and IPGP first, then savings and family support. Read those answers and be ready to say them. December to early February: the full bank (90+ questions); read every answer and be ready to say it in your own words.
  • 12. Questions to ask the labSunday, about 15 minutes: your one question for them. If you chose October, ask about the IPGP scholarship (deadline 13 Nov).
  • 13. Day-of checklistSunday, about 15 minutes: camera, mic, internet, backup.
  • 14. Email templatesMonday, within 30 minutes after the interview: write down every question they asked, then send the thank-you email in section 2. Later: any email to the lab.
